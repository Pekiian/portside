Portside

See what's listening on localhost. Open it, kill it, done.

View on GitHub → How it works

What it is

A native macOS menu bar app. It lists every TCP port reachable at http://localhost:PORT — nothing else. No LAN scanning, no remote hosts, no general network tool. Just your dev servers, one click away from opening or killing.

Features

Localhost only

Loopback and wildcard binds. LAN, link-local, and UDP are excluded. Dual-stack ports shown once.

Knows your servers

Tags Vite, Next, Node, Bun, Deno, Rails, Django, Postgres, Redis, Docker and more from the command line.

Workspace labels

Two storefronts? Rows show the workspace path so you can tell them apart at a glance.

Open or kill

Click to open in the browser, ⌘-click to copy the URL, one button to kill — SIGTERM, then SIGKILL after 3s.

Pin & rename

Pin ports to the top with a placeholder when they're down. Rename :5432 to "work db" inline.

Native & quiet

SwiftUI menu bar app, follows light/dark, sleep-aware polling. No dock icon, no chrome, no telemetry.

How it works

Reads locally

Runs lsof and ps on your machine to find listeners and their processes.

Filters & enriches

Keeps only localhost-reachable ports, then adds cwd, project name, runtime, and uptime.

Sends nothing

No network calls, no sandbox exception, no accounts. Everything stays on your Mac.
